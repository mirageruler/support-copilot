# Delivery

## 1. Delivery fees

The delivery fee depends on the distance between the restaurant and the delivery address: $1.50 for the first 2 km, plus $0.50 for each additional km, up to $4.00. The fee is shown at checkout before the customer pays and is part of the order value.

## 2. Delivery area

Restaurants deliver within 8 km. Addresses outside a restaurant's delivery area cannot be selected at checkout. Delivery areas can shrink for a while in heavy rain or when few riders are available.

## 3. Scheduled orders

Customers can schedule an order up to 3 days ahead, in 15-minute slots. A scheduled order can be changed or cancelled free of charge until the restaurant starts preparing it, usually 30 minutes before the slot.
