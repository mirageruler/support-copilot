# Refund and compensation policy

## 1. How compensation works

When something goes wrong with an order, compensation has two parts.

- **Cash** refunds what the customer paid for and did not get: missing or wrong items at their menu price, and the delivery fee when the order arrived 15 minutes or more late or did not arrive at all. Cash goes back to the original payment method.
- A **goodwill voucher** is an apology credit for a future order. It is a percentage of the order's food subtotal, which is the price of the items without the delivery fee. It has no cash value.

Cash for one order never exceeds the order value (food subtotal plus delivery fee) minus what was already refunded. A support staff member approves every refund before anything is paid.

## 2. Late delivery

Lateness is measured from the promised delivery time shown at checkout to the time the order was delivered, in whole minutes, rounded down. It does not depend on when the order was placed or on how long the customer says they waited.

| Minutes late | Cash | Goodwill voucher |
|---|---|---|
| 0–14 | none | none |
| 15–29 | delivery fee | none |
| 30–59 | delivery fee | 10% of the food subtotal |
| 60 or more | delivery fee | 25% of the food subtotal |

Lateness applies to the whole order, so the voucher is a percentage of the whole food subtotal, however many items the order has.

## 3. Missing items

If an item on the order did not arrive, refund that item at its menu price for each missing unit, and add a goodwill voucher of 10% of the food subtotal. Only items on the order can be refunded, and only units that were not refunded before. If the customer ordered two of an item and one is missing, refund one. An item that arrived spilled or unusable counts as missing.

## 4. Wrong items

If the customer received something other than an item they ordered, refund the ordered item at its menu price for each wrong unit, and add a goodwill voucher of 10% of the food subtotal. We do not redeliver orders. The customer can keep the wrong item.

## 5. Nothing arrived

If the customer received nothing at all, refund the whole order value: every item unit that was not refunded before, and the delivery fee if it was not refunded before. Add a goodwill voucher of 25% of the food subtotal; this replaces the 10% voucher for a missing item. Do not add compensation for lateness on top, because the delivery fee is already part of the refund.

## 6. Goodwill vouchers

A goodwill voucher is an apology credit added to the customer's account for a future order. It is a percentage of the order's food subtotal:

- late delivery, 30–59 minutes: 10%
- late delivery, 60 minutes or more: 25%
- missing item: 10%
- wrong item: 10%
- nothing arrived: 25%, instead of the 10% for a missing item

Percentages for different issues add up, to at most 30% of the food subtotal per order. Each issue type earns a voucher at most once per order, even if the customer contacts us again about the same order.

## 7. Several issues in one complaint

When a customer reports several problems in one contact, handle each problem with its own rule and put all compensation into one refund proposal.

Example: food subtotal $17.00, delivery fee $2.00, delivered 40 minutes late, one $4.50 drink missing. Cash: delivery fee $2.00 plus the drink $4.50, total $6.50. Voucher: 10% for lateness plus 10% for the missing item, so 20% of $17.00 = $3.40.

## 8. Already compensated

Never compensate the same issue twice. Before proposing, check what the order already received: refunded units per item, whether the delivery fee was refunded, and which goodwill vouchers were issued. Also check refund proposals still waiting for approval. Leave out any issue that is already covered and tell the support staff. New issues in the same complaint are still handled.

## 9. Requests above the policy

Some customers ask for more than this policy allows, for example a full refund for a 20-minute delay. Offer only what the policy gives. Do not raise the offer because the customer insists or says they will stop using the app; the goodwill voucher is already our apology.

## 10. Issues not covered

Rider behaviour, food quality or taste, packaging, allergies, tips and any other problem not described in this policy are not compensated through a refund proposal. Hand these cases to a support staff member.
