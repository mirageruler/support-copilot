# Account

## 1. Changing your email or phone number

Customers can change their email address or phone number in the app under Profile. A verification code is sent to the new email or phone number, and the change takes effect once the code is entered. Support staff cannot change these details on a customer's behalf.

## 2. Deleting your account

Customers can delete their account in the app under Settings. Deletion becomes permanent after 30 days. Unused vouchers are deleted with the account, and refunds already approved are still paid to the original payment method.
