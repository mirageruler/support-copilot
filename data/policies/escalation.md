# Escalation policy

## 1. Repeat claims

If the customer has 3 or more other orders that received compensation in the last 30 days, do not compensate this order. The order in the current complaint is not counted. Escalate the case to the risk team, whatever the complaint is about. In the reply, do not accuse the customer or mention fraud; say that a specialist team will review the case. An unreversed duplicate charge is still escalated to the payments team.

## 2. Escalating to the payments team

Escalate to the payments team when:

- an order has a duplicate charge that has not been reversed;
- a customer reports a double charge but only one payment is recorded;
- a cancelled order's charge was not reversed;
- a reversal or a temporary hold is 8 or more days old.

Include the order ID, the payment IDs involved and what the customer reported. Tell the customer the payments team will follow up; do not promise when it will be resolved.
