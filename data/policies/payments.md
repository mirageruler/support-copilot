# Payments and charges policy

## 1. Duplicate charges

A duplicate charge is two or more successful payments for the same order. The extra charge is the app's mistake, and only the payments team can reverse it; it is never refunded through a refund proposal.

Whenever an order has a duplicate charge that has not been reversed, escalate it to the payments team and tell the customer, even if the customer complained about something else. Handle the other problems with the order as usual.

If the duplicate was already reversed within the last 7 days, give the customer the reversal date and explain that the bank can take up to 7 days. If it was reversed 8 or more days ago and the customer has still not received it, escalate to the payments team.

## 2. Temporary holds

When a payment attempt fails, for example because the card was declined or the bank check timed out, the customer's bank may still show a pending amount for a few days. This is a temporary hold, not a charge.

If a customer says they were charged twice and the order has one failed and one successful payment, explain that the second amount is a hold that the bank releases on its own, usually within 7 days of the failed attempt. Make no refund. If the failed attempt was 8 or more days ago, escalate to the payments team. Do not mention holds the customer did not ask about.

## 3. One charge recorded

If a customer says they were charged twice but the order has only one payment, tell them we recorded one charge for this order, and escalate to the payments team so they can check with the bank. The second amount the customer sees may belong to another order.

## 4. Bank processing time

The payment system records the date when it reverses a charge, for a cancelled order or for a duplicate charge. Banks can take up to 7 days to show the money in the customer's account.

- Reversed within the last 7 days: give the customer the reversal date and explain the bank processing time.
- Reversed 8 or more days ago and the customer still has not received it: escalate to the payments team.

## 5. Cancelled orders

Cancelled orders are not compensated through refund proposals. When a customer asks about the money for a cancelled order:

- The charge was reversed: give the reversal date and explain that the bank can take up to 7 days. If it was reversed 8 or more days ago, escalate to the payments team.
- The charge was not reversed: escalate to the payments team.

Cancellation fees, and compensation for a long wait before an order was cancelled, are handled by a support staff member.
