# Membership

## 1. Membership benefits

The monthly membership costs $4.99. Members get 5% of the food subtotal back as a voucher after every delivered order, and early access to restaurant offers. Membership vouchers follow the same rules as other vouchers.

## 2. Cancelling a membership

Members can cancel at any time in the app. If they cancel within 7 days of being charged for a month, the membership fee for that month is refunded in full; otherwise the membership runs to the end of the paid month. Membership fees are not part of any order and are not refunded through order refunds.
